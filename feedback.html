01 — THE IMPORTANCE OF FEEDBACK

Improving Through Collaboration

Feedback is an important part of the design and development process. Having another person review a website can reveal usability issues, confusing content, or opportunities for improvement that might otherwise be overlooked.

For this project, I will ask a peer to review my career research website and provide feedback about its organization, navigation, content, and overall user experience. I will document the feedback, create a GitHub issue, make an appropriate change, and verify that the change works as intended.

05 — FINAL REFLECTION

Learning from the Review Process

The peer review process provides an opportunity to look at my work from another person's perspective. It helps identify areas where the website could be clearer, easier to navigate, or more useful to its intended audience.

Documenting feedback through GitHub also demonstrates how development teams can use issue tracking to organize improvements, communicate changes, and verify results.

Once the review is complete, I will reflect on what I learned and how the feedback influenced the final website.
