MY PROFESSIONAL DIRECTION

Professional Goals

Defining the skills, experiences, and career aspirations that will shape my future in technology.

01 — PROFESSIONAL IDENTITY

Technology with a Human-Centered Perspective

My professional interests are centered around the intersection of software development, artificial intelligence, and user experience design. As a Human-Computer Interaction student, I am particularly interested in how technology can be designed and developed to solve problems while providing meaningful and intuitive experiences for people.

I want to build a strong technical foundation while also understanding the people who use the technology I help create. This combination allows me to explore different career paths, including Forward Deployed Engineering and AI Engineering and Implementation.

02 — SHORT-TERM GOALS

Building My Foundation

My immediate goal is to strengthen my technical knowledge, gain practical experience, and develop projects that demonstrate my abilities.

💻

Improve Programming Skills

Continue developing my knowledge of programming languages, object-oriented programming, software development, and problem-solving.

🤖

Explore Artificial Intelligence

Learn more about generative AI, large language models, AI agents, APIs, and how intelligent applications are developed and integrated.

🎨

Develop UX and Design Experience

Continue learning about UX research, usability testing, interface design, prototyping, and human-centered development.

🚀

Build Practical Projects

Create personal and academic projects that demonstrate my technical abilities, creativity, and ability to solve real-world problems.

04 — LONG-TERM GOALS

Where I Want to Go

My long-term goal is to establish a career in technology where I can contribute to developing useful, innovative, and intelligent products.

Forward Deployed Engineering

I am interested in eventually working in a role that combines software engineering, AI integration, and direct collaboration with customers. I would like to help organizations implement technical solutions that address their specific needs.

AI Engineering and Implementation

I am also interested in developing and implementing AI-powered applications, automation systems, and intelligent tools. I would like to contribute to making AI useful and accessible in different industries.

05 — MY PROFESSIONAL VISION

Bringing Technology and People Together

I do not see software development, artificial intelligence, and UX design as completely separate areas. I see them as complementary skills that can work together to create better products and experiences.

Software development provides the technical foundation, artificial intelligence introduces new capabilities, and human-centered design helps ensure that the resulting technology addresses actual user needs.

By continuing to develop knowledge in all three areas, I hope to build a versatile skill set that allows me to contribute to different types of technology projects and pursue opportunities across the software and AI industries.

"Building intelligent technology with people in mind."
