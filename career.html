CAREER RESEARCH

Exploring Career Opportunities

Researching careers that combine software development, artificial intelligence, automation, and technology.

My Career Interests

I am interested in pursuing a career that combines software development, artificial intelligence, and human-centered technology. Through my research, I explored opportunities in Forward Deployed Engineering and AI Engineering and Implementation.

I selected three job postings from different companies to better understand the responsibilities, technical skills, and qualifications associated with these roles.

FORWARD DEPLOYED ENGINEERING

Forward Deployed Engineer

Figma
01

Figma is a software company known for its collaborative design and product development platform. Its Forward Deployed Engineering team works directly with enterprise customers to integrate Figma products into their existing technical environments.

Key Responsibilities

  • Integrate Figma products into customer environments.
  • Work with enterprise codebases and design systems.
  • Develop AI-powered workflows and agent integrations.
  • Troubleshoot technical and integration problems.
  • Work with customers to understand technical needs.

Technical Skills and Tools

APIs and SDKs LLMs and AI Agents MCP Prompt Engineering Git and CI Design Systems

Location and Work Arrangement

United States. The position can be performed remotely or from one of Figma's U.S. hubs, with periodic travel to work with customers.

Qualifications and Compensation

This is a senior/staff-level engineering position requiring at least five years of experience shipping production software. The posting emphasizes strong engineering fundamentals, customer-facing technical communication, design systems, TypeScript/JavaScript or Python, APIs, SDKs, and AI-powered workflows. The listed annual base salary range is $153,000–$376,000.

Why This Career Interests Me

This position interests me because it combines software development, AI, and collaboration with customers. I also find the connection between technical implementation and product design particularly interesting.

AI AND AUTOMATION

Junior AI and Automation Engineer

Myriad360
02

Myriad360 is an IT services and consulting company. Its AI and Automation team develops tools and applications that use artificial intelligence and automation to improve everyday business operations.

Key Responsibilities

  • Build and maintain AI-powered applications.
  • Create and improve workflow automations.
  • Configure GPTs, AI agents, and prompts.
  • Connect applications through APIs and integrations.
  • Test solutions and document technical processes.
  • Work with stakeholders and incorporate feedback.

Technical Skills and Tools

Python APIs JSON Generative AI Power Automate StackAI Zapier Microsoft 365

Location and Work Arrangement

Applicants must be based in the United States. Travel of up to 10% may be required. The posting does not clearly specify a particular office arrangement.

Qualifications and Compensation

The position requests 0–2 years of relevant experience. Coursework, internships, and personal projects count. It emphasizes foundational programming skills, preferably Python, and familiarity with APIs, JSON, and generative AI.

The listed base salary for New York City is $85,000. Compensation in other locations may vary.

Why This Career Interests Me

This role is particularly interesting because it provides an opportunity to apply programming and AI skills to practical problems. It also offers exposure to automation platforms, AI agents, and real-world software development.

AI SOLUTIONS ENGINEERING

AI Solutions Engineer

Humanscale
03

Humanscale is a manufacturer of ergonomic workplace products, including office seating, desks, and technology accessories. Its AI Solutions Engineer position focuses on applying artificial intelligence to improve business processes and operations.

Key Responsibilities

  • Identify business processes that could benefit from AI.
  • Develop AI-powered applications and prototypes.
  • Build workflow automations and intelligent tools.
  • Integrate AI solutions with existing business systems.
  • Evaluate AI solutions and measure their effectiveness.
  • Collaborate with technical teams and business stakeholders.

Technical Skills and Tools

Python Azure AI Azure OpenAI Power Automate Copilot Studio APIs SQL RAG Streamlit

Location and Work Arrangement

Piscataway, New Jersey. The position requires working onsite at least three days per week.

Qualifications and Compensation

The posting requests a bachelor's degree in a related field or equivalent practical experience. It emphasizes programming experience, familiarity with AI-powered applications and integrations, and the ability to develop practical solutions for business needs. The posting does not specify a minimum number of years of professional experience.

Listed salary range: $105,541–$153,943. The position requires U.S. work authorization without current or future visa sponsorship.

Why This Career Interests Me

This opportunity interests me because it demonstrates how AI can be applied outside traditional software companies. I find the idea of developing intelligent solutions for real business needs especially appealing.

WHAT I LEARNED

My Career Direction

Through this research, I discovered that Forward Deployed Engineering and AI Engineering share several important skills, including programming, problem-solving, technical communication, and understanding how software can address real-world needs.

However, the positions differ in their emphasis. Forward Deployed Engineering involves working closely with customers and integrating technology into existing environments, while AI Engineering focuses more directly on developing and implementing intelligent applications and automation.

I am interested in both directions and would like to continue developing skills that could prepare me for either path. My background and interests in Human-Computer Interaction also make UX and human-centered design important parts of my professional direction.
