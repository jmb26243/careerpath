PORTFOLIO PLANNING

Designing My Professional Portfolio

Exploring visual directions and making design decisions that reflect my professional identity and career goals.

01 — TARGET AUDIENCE

Designing for Recruiters and Hiring Managers

My primary audience will be recruiters and hiring managers who are interested in learning about my education, technical abilities, projects, and professional interests.

Because visitors may have limited time, the portfolio should make important information easy to find. It should communicate my interests in software development, artificial intelligence, and Human-Computer Interaction while presenting my projects and experience in an organized way.

Easy navigation Visitors should find information quickly.
Clear project presentation Projects should demonstrate skills and results.
Professional appearance The design should be polished and consistent.

03 — FINAL DESIGN DECISION

A Hybrid of Both Directions

I decided to combine the strongest elements of both concepts to create a portfolio that is professional, approachable, and visually engaging.

SELECTED DESIGN

Modern Professional + Creative Technology

The final direction will use the clean layout and easy navigation of Direction A, while incorporating selected creative visuals and interactive elements from Direction B.

Professional Structure

Clear sections, organized project cards, and consistent navigation.

Creative Presentation

Engaging project previews, subtle animations, and visual storytelling.

Balanced Experience

A polished design that is visually interesting without overwhelming visitors.

04 — DESIGN SPECIFICATIONS

Final Design Decisions

These decisions will guide the visual appearance and functionality of my future professional portfolio.

Color Palette

Light blue, white, black, and pale blue.

Typography

Modern, readable sans-serif fonts such as Inter or Roboto.

Layout

Clean, organized, responsive, and balanced.

Navigation

Simple navigation with clearly labeled sections.

Project Presentation

Visual project cards with descriptions, screenshots, tools, and project details.

Interactions

Subtle hover effects, smooth transitions, and useful interactive elements.

Accessibility

Readable text, sufficient color contrast, descriptive links, and responsive layouts.

05 — DESIGN RATIONALE

Why I Chose This Direction

I chose a hybrid design because I want my portfolio to appeal to recruiters and hiring managers while still reflecting my creativity and interest in technology. A clean and organized layout will make it easier for visitors to find information about my background, skills, and projects.

At the same time, visual project previews and subtle interactive elements will help communicate my interest in software development, AI, and UX design. The light blue, white, and black color palette provides a modern appearance without making the website feel too busy.

Overall, I want the portfolio to communicate professionalism, creativity, and an interest in building technology with people in mind.

Professional in structure. Creative in presentation. Human-centered in purpose.
